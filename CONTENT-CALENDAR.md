## Content Quality Standards

- Minimum length: 800 words (blog), 600 words (service page), 400 words (area page)
- Must include: primary keyword in H1, first 100 words, one H2
- Must include: London-specific context (areas, landmarks, business types)
- Must include: CTA to contact/quote at end of every page
- Must include: Schema markup (Article for blog, Service for service pages)
- Must include: Internal links to 2+ related service/area pages
- Images: All must have descriptive alt text with keyword
